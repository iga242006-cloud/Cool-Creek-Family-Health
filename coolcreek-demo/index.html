Why direct primary care

Primary care, the way it should feel

No insurance billing. No waiting rooms full of strangers. Just a small practice in the heart of Carmel with time to actually take care of you.

Time to be heard

Our visits aren't built around billing codes, so they aren't rushed. Bring your whole list of questions — and get same- and next-day access when something comes up.

One honest price

A flat monthly membership covers your primary care — annual labs, in-house testing, minor procedures, and EKGs included. No copays, no surprise bills, ever.

Root-cause medicine

We practice lifestyle medicine in Carmel: nutrition, sleep, stress, and gut health — and thoughtful deprescribing — to treat the cause, not just the symptom.

Your provider

Meet Farah Myers, FNP, DipACLM

Farah is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner and a diplomate of the American College of Lifestyle Medicine, raised right down the road in Columbus, Indiana. She founded Cool Creek Family Health to escape rushed, insurance-driven medicine and build the kind of practice where healing actually has room to happen.

Alongside Keyla Matthews, FNP — a bilingual nurse practitioner with a critical care background — and collaborating physician Dr. Jessica Saberman, Farah cares for a deliberately small panel of patients across Carmel and Hamilton County.
