Direct primary care, explained

What is DPC, exactly?

Direct primary care is a return to the original doctor-patient relationship. Instead of billing insurance for every visit and test, your provider works directly for you — funded by one flat membership fee.

That single change removes the middleman that makes conventional medicine feel rushed and expensive. No coding requirements dictating your visit length. No claim denials. No mystery bills three months later.

  • Your provider answers to you — not an insurance company
  • Visits as long as your health actually requires
  • A deliberately small patient panel across Hamilton County
  • Transparent pricing on everything, inside membership and out
  • Pairs with high-deductible, catastrophic, or health-share plans

Membership FAQ

We recommend it — just a different kind. Membership covers your day-to-day primary care, and most members pair it with a high-deductible, catastrophic, or health-share plan for emergencies, hospitalizations, and specialists. We don't bill insurance for anything, which is exactly why we can keep visits long and pricing simple.

Many of our members use HSA or FSA funds toward their membership, and the rules in this area continue to evolve in DPC's favor. Because everyone's tax situation is different, we recommend confirming with your tax advisor or plan administrator — and we're happy to provide any documentation you need.

We prescribe thoughtfully and conservatively. Controlled substances are prescribed only when clinically appropriate and within Indiana regulations, and we are not a pain-management practice. Our lifestyle-medicine approach often focuses on the opposite: safely deprescribing medications you may no longer need.
