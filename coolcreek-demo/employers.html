For employers

The benefits alternative your team will actually thank you for

Cool Creek Family Health partners with small businesses across Carmel and Hamilton County to offer direct primary care as an employee benefit — predictable for you, genuinely valuable for them.

The problem

Premiums keep climbing. Care keeps shrinking.

Small businesses pay more every year for plans employees barely use — because deductibles are so high that a visit still feels unaffordable, so problems wait until they're expensive.

Predictable cost

One flat monthly rate per employee. No claims, no utilization surprises, no annual renewal shock. Budgeting your primary care line item becomes simple arithmetic.

A benefit people feel

Employees get a real provider who knows their name, same- and next-day visits, included labs and procedures, and zero copays. It's the rare benefit that gets used — and talked about.

Less time away from work

Quick access and direct messaging mean fewer half-day disappearances for a 10-minute appointment — and small issues get handled before they become absences.

How it fits

DPC slots neatly into your existing benefits

Direct primary care isn't insurance — it's the layer that makes your insurance work harder. Pair memberships with a high-deductible or level-funded plan and your team gets everyday care covered up front, while the plan handles the big stuff.

  • Works alongside high-deductible, level-funded, and health-share plans
  • Covers the primary care your team uses most — visits, labs, testing, minor procedures
  • Month-to-month flexibility as your headcount changes
  • Simple onboarding — we handle enrollment and meet every employee personally
What your employees get

Membership, on the company

  • Unhurried visits with a board-certified nurse practitioner
  • Same- and next-day appointments
  • Annual labs and in-house testing included
  • Minor procedures and EKGs at no extra cost
  • Lifestyle medicine for long-term health, not just sick visits
  • Bilingual care in English and Spanish
