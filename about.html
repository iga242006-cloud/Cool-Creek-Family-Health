About us

The people behind the practice

A family nurse practitioner-led team in Carmel, Indiana, built around one belief: you deserve a provider who knows you — and has time for you.

Our founder

Farah Myers, FNP, DipACLM

Farah grew up in Columbus, Indiana, and has spent her career learning what good medicine looks like — and what it doesn't. After years inside the conventional system, she kept running into the same wall: seven-minute visits, treatment driven by insurance codes, and patients leaving with more prescriptions but no more answers.

She founded Cool Creek Family Health to practice the way she was trained to care: unhurried visits, real relationships, and lifestyle medicine that works on root causes — nutrition, sleep, stress management, gut health, and thoughtfully deprescribing medications that are no longer serving you.

  • Board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner
  • DipACLM — Diplomate, American College of Lifestyle Medicine
  • BS, Biology — Indiana University
  • MSN — DePaul University
  • FNP certification — Frontier Nursing University
Why “Cool Creek”?

Named for the creek up the road

Cool Creek winds quietly through Carmel and Westfield, just up the road from our Rangeline Road office. It's where our neighbors walk, unwind, and slow down — and that's exactly the pace we wanted our medicine to keep. Steady. Unhurried. Part of the community it runs through.
